Ecliptic — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Ecliptic is an Android app that inspects the technology stack of a website you choose to scan. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with data. The short version: Ecliptic has no user accounts, collects no personal information, and runs no analytics or advertising.

Information we collect

None about you. Ecliptic does not create accounts, does not ask for your name, email, or any identifier, and does not include any analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on any server. We operate no backend server for the app.

Data stored on your device

Your scan history (the URLs you scan and the results) is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. It never leaves your device except through features you explicitly trigger (for example, exporting or sharing a report). Uninstalling the app removes this data.

Network requests made when you scan a site

Scanning is the core function of the app and requires contacting the internet. When you scan a URL, the app sends requests to the following, using the address or domain you entered:

  • The website you scan — loaded in an embedded browser (WebView) and fetched over HTTP(S) to read its headers, HTML, scripts, and cookies.
  • Google Public DNS (dns.google) — to look up the site's DNS records.
  • RDAP / WHOIS (rdap.net, rdap.iana.org) — to look up domain registration details.
  • ipinfo.io — to look up the hosting network/ASN for the site's IP address.
  • crt.sh — certificate-transparency logs, to enumerate the domain's subdomains.
  • HackerTarget (api.hackertarget.com) — reverse-IP lookup, to find other sites sharing the server's IP address.
  • jsDelivr CDN (cdn.jsdelivr.net) and Simple Icons (cdn.simpleicons.org) — to load technology and brand icons shown in results.
  • Google Fonts — to load the app's fonts.
  • Google Play — only if an in-app review prompt is shown.

The domain or IP address you choose to scan is shared with the DNS, RDAP/WHOIS, and IP-info services above solely to perform those lookups. Each of those third parties handles requests under its own privacy policy. Ecliptic does not send them any information about you.

Permissions

The app requests a single permission: Internet access, required to perform scans. It does not request access to contacts, location, camera, storage, or any other sensitive permission.

Sharing and sale of data

We do not sell, rent, or share any data. There is no data about you to share.

Children

Ecliptic is a developer/security utility and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.
